# Three Run-Throughs

These are the intended demo results using the buttons in the app.

## Priya, 29

Questions asked: age, city tier, salaried employment, monthly income, income stability, variable income share, existing EMI, expenses, emergency savings, credit score, recent bounces, purpose, requested amount, tenure and quote if any.

Inputs: Bengaluru salaried software engineer, income ₹1,10,000, no other household income counted, existing car EMI ₹14,000, rent/expenses ₹28,000, score 780, wants ₹8,00,000 personal loan for 36 months.

Outputs:

- Verdict: Borrow.
- Lender may sanction: around ₹8,00,000.
- Borrower-safe amount: around ₹8,00,000.
- Fair interest rate: about 11.8-18.6%; all-in APR about 14.5-22.5%.
- EMI ceiling: around ₹38,500.

Negotiation Card: Ask for the full ₹8 lakh only if the EMI stays under roughly ₹38.5k, push a fair-rate argument below the upper band because her score is strong, and compare the APR after fees.

## Ravi, 42

Questions asked: self-employed income, income range/stability, household income, expenses, existing EMIs, credit score if known, collateral, product purpose, requested amount and tenure.

Inputs: Mysuru kirana owner, cash income ₹40,000-80,000 so app uses ₹58,000, no formal loan, no score, wife earns ₹18,000 so 75% is counted as dependable household income, shop premises value ₹45,00,000, wants ₹15,00,000 for stock line and delivery vehicle over 60 months.

Outputs:

- Verdict: Borrow less.
- Lender may sanction: around ₹9,65,000 because collateral is strong but cash income and unknown score widen the range.
- Borrower-safe amount: around ₹7,50,000.
- Fair interest rate: about 16.0-25.4%; all-in APR about 18.9-30.5%.
- EMI ceiling: around ₹22,000.

Negotiation Card: Route him to a secured business/LAP-style product rather than an unsecured personal loan. Ask for a smaller first tranche tied to stock turnover and vehicle earnings, and require APR disclosure including fees.

## Anita, 35

Questions asked: informal income, volatility, existing app loans, expenses, savings, credit score if known, recent bounce, two-wheeler collateral/purpose, amount and tenure.

Inputs: Hubballi delivery rider and home tailor, income ₹26,000-30,000 so app uses ₹30,000, existing app-loan EMI ₹35,000, no score, recent bounced EMI, wants ₹1,50,000 electric scooter loan at quoted 30%+.

Outputs:

- Verdict: Don't borrow.
- Lender may sanction: close to ₹0 after existing EMI and repayment-risk checks.
- Borrower-safe amount: ₹0.
- Fair interest rate: about 15-24%; all-in APR about 16-26%; a 30% quote is above fair range.
- EMI ceiling: ₹0.

Negotiation Card: Do not take the new scooter loan now. First restructure/close app loans, build one month of buffer and return when total EMI is below the ceiling. A productive scooter can help only after current debt stress is repaired.
